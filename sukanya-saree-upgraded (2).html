Sukanya Saree. Scroll to travel along one continuous drape, from the looms of Varanasi to the knotted dots of Bandhani.

Scroll to explore

Banarasi

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Brocade silk woven with zari, the metallic thread that gives the cloth its glow. Look for dense floral jaal and a broad gold border.

Kanjivaram

Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu

Heavy, lustrous silk with a border in a bold contrasting colour. Temple zigzags, stripes and paisleys are the signatures.

Chanderi

Chanderi, Madhya Pradesh

Featherlight and slightly sheer, woven from silk and cotton. Small coin motifs float on a fine, glowing ground.

Bandhani

Gujarat and Rajasthan

Cloth is pinched into thousands of tiny knots and dyed, leaving clusters of pale dots against deep colour.
